`manifest.json` records each icon's ID, descriptive name, exact source URL, and SHA-256. The SVG files are unmodified. The renderer adapts their presentation with stroke tracing and opacity animation. Those visual adaptations are offered under CC BY-SA 4.0 and carry attribution in the video and companion project.

The fixture's candy icon is a visual symbol for sugar molecules; it does not mean that plants manufacture wrapped candy.
